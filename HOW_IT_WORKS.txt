==============================================================================
  PART A — HOW I WORK (the improvement loop)
==============================================================================

I'm not just "writing code." I run a continuous research loop, like a quant
research department. The loop is:

   1. OBSERVE      Look at the real running system + the database of past trades.
   2. MEASURE      Pull hard numbers (never guess). "72% of buys are red in 5 min."
   3. HYPOTHESIZE  Form a theory about WHY. "We're buying coins that already ran."
   4. EXPERIMENT   Backtest the fix on recorded data, OR run it in "shadow" first.
   5. IMPLEMENT    Ship the change — gated/off-by-default or shadow when risky.
   6. VERIFY       Watch it in the REAL running app. Screenshots, DB, live logs.
   7. KEEP/REVERT  If the measured result improves, keep it. If not, revert.
   8. RE-AUDIT     Ask "what's the next biggest bottleneck?" -> back to step 1.

It never really "finishes." There's no stop condition except you telling me to
stop. After every fix I look for the next highest-impact problem.

THE RULES I HOLD MYSELF TO (this is the important part):

  * EVIDENCE, NOT VIBES. I only say "Verified" when I've actually observed it in
    the running product (a number in your DB, a screenshot, a live log line). If
    I haven't proven it, I say "Not Verified." I never say "should work."

  * I WON'T FOOL YOU. The worst outcome isn't "we couldn't make it profitable" —
    it's me telling you it's profitable when it isn't. So I keep telling you the
    parts that DON'T work yet. If a change is still losing, I say so.

  * FALSIFIABLE CLAIMS. When I ship something, I attach a number it has to beat,
    and I commit to reverting if it doesn't. BS can't be tested; my claims can.

  * MEASURE BEFORE BELIEVING. Example: a teammate-style idea "sell if it's red at
    15 min" sounded smart, but when I tested it on real data it was WORSE (it cut
    the dip-then-rip winners). So I did NOT ship it. Intuition gets tested.

  * SAFETY IS FIXED, NOT NEGOTIABLE. The engine NEVER holds a private key, never
    signs, never auto-executes a real trade. It produces SIGNALS. You trade for
    real manually; "paper trading" is just a fake-wallet simulator to test the
    exact same logic safely. And I never lower the BUY conviction gate (a past
    replay proved a loose gate lost ~136 SOL).

HOW YOU SEE THE LOOP RUNNING:
  - The engine runs in the background, scoring coins and paper-trading.
  - I write little read-only "probe" scripts (in scripts/research/) that query
    your database and print the truth. You can run any of them yourself.
  - The dashboard (http://127.0.0.1:3000) shows live decisions, paper PnL, the
    reasoning feed, and the Profit x Time chart.


==============================================================================
  PART B — HOW THE ENGINE WORKS (find -> decide -> exit)
==============================================================================

STEP 1: FIND A COIN (discovery)
  - Original source: it listens to pump.fun and sees brand-new coins the second
    they're born (free feed).
  - NEW this session: a "maturing-survivor" scanner that watches DexScreener +
    GeckoTerminal for coins that already GRADUATED (have a real trading pool,
    enough liquidity, recent, active). The idea: a 1-hour-old coin with real
    data is a far better bet than a 5-second-old one we're blind on.

STEP 2: OBSERVE (~90 seconds)
  - It watches the coin for a bit, pulling free DexScreener data (price moves,
    buy/sell counts, volume).

STEP 3: SAFETY GATE (hard kill)
  - Checks for rug signals: can the dev mint more supply? freeze your tokens?
    is one wallet holding most of it? is it a honeypot (can't sell)?
  - If it's clearly bad -> AVOID, full stop. Nothing overrides safety.

STEP 4: SCORE -> CONVICTION (0-100)
  - Several "facets" each give a score: organic volume, momentum, graduation,
    smart-money, social/hype, etc. They're blended into one conviction number.
  - Gates/caps apply: too little real evidence -> capped to "WATCH" only.

STEP 5: VERDICT
  - conviction >= 55 -> BUY_SMALL ;  >= 72 -> BUY_STRONG ;  else WATCH or AVOID.
  - There's also a "don't chase" check: if the coin already ran up a lot, hold off.

STEP 6: SIZE (MiroFish risk engine)
  - Decides how big the (paper) position should be, based on conviction, safety,
    liquidity, and "market weather" (is the engine winning lately?). If the
    engine is losing, it shrinks every bet to a floor — a safety brake.

STEP 7: EXIT (this is 80% of the game)
  - Profit ladder: take some off as it rises (e.g. 1.4x, 1.8x, 2.5x...).
  - Trailing stop: lock in gains once it's up, sell if it falls back from the peak.
  - Stop-loss: cut a loser fast.
  - Time stop: don't hold forever.


==============================================================================
  PART C — WHAT I FOUND & CHANGED THIS SESSION + HONEST STATE
==============================================================================

THE CORE PROBLEM I PROVED (with your own data, ~19k signals):
  - The engine was BUYING TOPS. The typical buy was a coin already up ~70%
    (median), and 72% of buys were underwater within 5 minutes.
  - 100% of coins that eventually doubled had first crashed 45%+ — so the
    "stop-loss" sold them before they could moon. We were optimizing a target
    (peak price) the exit could never actually capture.
  - The "don't chase" guard existed but had NEVER fired once in 19,000 decisions,
    because it was reading an empty data feed.
  - The position sizer (MiroFish) was pinned at its tiny floor — partly CORRECT
    (it detected we were losing and protected the wallet), partly a bug (it
    punished coins for missing data that's structurally unavailable on newborns).

WHAT I SHIPPED (all tested, most verified live):
  1. Instrumented entry timing + a "don't chase" guard fed REAL run-up data.
  2. Reshaped momentum so it stops rewarding already-pumped spikes.
  3. Better exit ladder (harvest earlier) + tighter stop — backtested less-bad.
  4. Fixed MiroFish's structural penalties so sizing works on mature coins.
  5. Fixed the paper PnL display so balance + PnL actually reconcile to equity
     (it was showing inconsistent numbers before).
  6. Built the maturing-survivor scanner (running now in SHADOW: it journals
     candidates but doesn't paper-buy them yet, so we can prove they're better
     BEFORE risking on them).

THE HONEST STATE:

    real fix is to stop being LATE.
  - The research (and your own instinct) agree: meme money comes from catching a
